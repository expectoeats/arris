Who we are

Who we are

Arris Architects

At Arris Architects, we believe in the dual pillars of client service and design excellence. Across education, cinema and commercial developments, we bring together design thinking, technical rigour and an understanding of how spaces are experienced. Our work lies in balancing complex parameters and always going a step further every time.


Through considered planning and integrated design, we create spaces that respond to their context, enrich everyday experiences and retain their relevance over time. For us, good design is not simply about what is built, but how it is experienced.
