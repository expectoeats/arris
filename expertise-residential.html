Crafting personal sanctuaries that celebrate lifestyle and luxury.

A home is a reflection of the people who live within it. At Arris, we approach residential design with deep empathy and creative precision. We create bespoke living spaces that balance privacy with openness, luxury with comfort, and timeless elegance with modern functionality.

Residential

Architecture for the art of living.

Our residential expertise spans from high-end private villas to luxury apartment complexes. We focus on the flow of daily life, ensuring that every room is intuitive and purposeful. We integrate indoor and outdoor living to create a seamless connection with the environment, prioritizing natural light, ventilation, and sustainable design.

Every detail is meticulously curated—from the selection of premium materials to the integration of smart home technologies. We work closely with our clients to understand their unique needs and aspirations, translating them into architectural forms that are both beautiful and highly functional.

Arris is dedicated to creating residences that are not just places to live, but legacies to be cherished. We design for longevity, ensuring that our homes remain relevant and inspiring for generations to come.
