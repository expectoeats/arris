Our Team

2018

Evolving Through Exploration

2018

Architecture is constantly changing, and so are the possibilities within it. We continued to explore new materials, technologies, processes, and approaches while remaining grounded in the fundamentals of good design. Research and experimentation became integral to the practice, allowing ideas to evolve beyond conventional solutions.

2020

A New Perspective

2020

A rapidly changing world brought new questions about how people live, work, interact, and experience space. We responded by looking more closely at adaptability, resilience, sustainability, technology, and human well-being. The period became an opportunity to reconsider established approaches and explore what architecture could mean in a changing environment.

2025

A Legacy in Progress

2025

Twenty years of practice represent more than a measure of time. They represent countless conversations, ideas, drawings, decisions, collaborations, challenges, and completed spaces. Our legacy is reflected in the work we have created and the relationships we have built, while our ambition remains focused on what lies ahead.
