Understanding

We begin by understanding the aspirations of the client, the requirements of the project and the realities of the site. This allows us to establish a clear design direction rooted in both purpose and possibility.

  • Client Aspirations

    Understanding the vision, expectations and ambitions behind the project.

  • Program Requirements

    Defining the functions, relationships and spatial requirements.

  • Site & Context

    Reading the physical, cultural and environmental character of the place.

  • Regulations

    Working within planning, statutory and development frameworks.

The Arris Approach

Thoughtful by Process. Distinctive by Design.

Our process is structured, but never rigid. Every site, client, programme, and context brings a different set of opportunities. We allow the design to evolve through investigation and dialogue, while maintaining a clear focus on functionality, character, sustainability, and experience.

Context. Function. Material. Light. Experience.

Together, they shape architecture that is considered in process and lasting in presence.
