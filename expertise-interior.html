© 2026 Arris Architects

We shape spaces into stories that shape the way people connect.

At Arris, interior design extends beyond the arrangement of spaces and finishes. It is about understanding what a space seeks to express and the emotions it intends to evoke. Through spatial composition, visual cues, materiality and light, we explore how interiors can influence perception, guide movement and shape the way people interact with their surroundings. Whether it is a school that encourages curiosity, a cinema that builds anticipation, a residence that feels personal, or an entertainment space that invites engagement, each interior begins with the story it seeks to tell.

Interior

Curating spaces that inspire, comfort, and endure

Preparing proposal layouts for over 150 cinema screens, our expertise in interior design comes naturally. With a deep understanding of cinematic spaces, we create environments that seamlessly blend plush interiors, glamour, functionality, and contemporary aesthetics. Every project is approached with a strong focus on spatial planning, material selection, detailing, and the overall experience of the audience.

Our design language brings together modern materials, refined finishes, innovative concepts, and efficient execution. Backed by fast, skilled workmanship and closely supervised on-site execution, we ensure that every element is translated from concept to reality with precision and consistency. From luxurious cinema interiors to thoughtfully designed public and hospitality spaces, our work reflects a careful balance between visual impact and practical performance.
