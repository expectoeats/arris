Hospitality

At Arris Architects, our hospitality design experience spans hotel lobbies, themed cafés and lounges, and F&Bs within commercial developments, cinemas and entertainment destinations. We approach each space through its intended atmosphere and identity, exploring how spatial planning, materials, lighting and detailing can shape the guest experience.


From welcoming lobby spaces to distinctive F&B interiors and relaxed lounges, our focus lies in creating inviting environments that balance visual character, comfort, functionality and the operational needs of each establishment.
