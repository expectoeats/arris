What they're Saying

Honourable Shri Manish Jaiswal MoP

DPS Hzb

We have been associated with Shubhashish since 2001, when he was a young Project Architect working on my multiplex project in Surat. Even then, his confidence, design capability, and commitment stood out clearly.

When we decided to take up our first school project, Angel High School, in 2007, we had no hesitation in approaching Arris Architects. What he presented to us was far beyond our expectations — a concept truly ahead of its time. His ideas on school design and campus planning were visionary. The project, executed in phases, continues to evolve even today, and AHS has become a landmark in our city.

Later, when the DPS Society approached us, we once again turned to Shubhashish to design the new school within the adjacent campus — but with a clear brief that the two schools must retain distinct identities. He responded with a design that was graceful, dignified, and worthy of the DPS brand.

Today, we feel proud owners of both these institutions. The thoughtful designs have positively impacted students, teachers, and parents alike — and as stakeholders, the projects have brought us immense respect and goodwill within the community.

Avneet Uberoi

Bottle House

When we decided to transform what was once an iconic building into our home, we knew it was not going to be an ordinary architectural project. We wanted to preserve the character and memories associated with the building while creating a space that truly felt like ours.

From our first conversation, Shubhashish understood that we were looking for more than just a beautifully designed house. We wanted a home that was sophisticated, functional and timeless, while still retaining the soul of the original structure. He brought all of that to life.

The Bottle House is now a home we are deeply proud of — every room tells a story, and every corner reflects thoughtful design.
