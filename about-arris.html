© 2026 Arris Architects

Established in 2004, Arris Architects specialises in creating larger than life experiences and has shaped a wide range of buildings and spaces from shopping malls and multiplexes to educational institutions, high-end residential complexes, private residences and corporate offices.


At the heart of our practice lies the ability to interpret a client's vision with clarity and translate it into purposeful design. Backed by 25+ years of foundational experience, we combine creative thinking and an understanding of practical realities to deliver high-quality design solutions that balance ambition, cost efficiency and time.

Design
Philosophy

At Arris Architects, design begins with an understanding of context, purpose and the people who inhabit a space. We approach every project as an opportunity to look beyond the obvious, translating our clients’ aspirations into architecture that is thoughtful and enduring.

Each design decision prioritises clarity of thought, technical nitty gritty and an understanding of how a space must function, feel and perform. For us, design involvement is of utmost importance for a seamless transition of ideas into technology and technology into reality. We strive to evolve over and above the brief by always re-inventing the space in terms of material, attributes and concept of design derivations.

Design
Process

Our architectural design process begins with a thorough understanding of the client's vision, project brief, site conditions, functional requirements and long-term objectives. Through detailed discussions, site analysis and collaborative planning, we establish a design strategy tailored to each project.

Our integrated approach to architecture, interior design and technical planning ensures that every solution responds to the specific demands of cinema and multiplex design, educational architecture, commercial developments, residential design and interior design projects.

From concept development and architectural planning to design detailing, engineering coordination and project execution, we follow an iterative process driven by continuous client communication and feedback. Each stage involves careful evaluation, design refinement and coordination to balance aesthetics, functionality, technical feasibility and performance. This structured yet collaborative approach enables Arris Architects to deliver well-coordinated architectural and interior design solutions that align with client expectations and project objectives.
